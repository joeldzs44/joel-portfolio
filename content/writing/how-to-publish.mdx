The first paragraph of a post renders as a lead. Keep it to two or three sentences that tell the reader what they will learn.

<Chapter label="Frontmatter" title="Every post starts with a few fields.">

The file name becomes the URL, so `how-to-publish.mdx` is served at `/writing/how-to-publish`. Required fields are `title`, `description`, `standfirst`, `date` and `category`. Optional fields are `tags`, `draft`, `related` and `cover`.

Categories become the filters on the writing index automatically — there is no list to maintain.

</Chapter>
